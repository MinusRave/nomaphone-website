## Where DialAnyone Wins

Let's be direct about DialAnyone's advantages.

### Price

Six times cheaper on US calls. For high-volume callers, that adds up. If you're making hours of calls per month, the savings are meaningful.

### Virtual Numbers in 50+ Countries

Need a local number in Germany, Japan, or Brazil? DialAnyone has you covered. NomaPhone offers virtual numbers for the US and Canada only. If you need a local presence in multiple countries, DialAnyone is the obvious choice.

### eSIM Data ($4.99/mo)

This is clever bundling. If you're a nomad who needs both calling and mobile data, getting both from one provider simplifies your setup. NomaPhone doesn't offer data at all.

### Full API with Webhooks

If you're a developer building something — an app that makes calls, an automated system, a customer support tool — DialAnyone's REST API gives you programmable voice. NomaPhone has no API. This isn't even a comparison for developer use cases.

### Open Source

DialAnyone's codebase is open source. They claim full encryption and privacy. If code transparency matters to you (and it should), this is a genuine advantage. You can audit what they're doing with your data.

### SMS and 2FA

Both services support SMS and 2FA. This is a tie. Banks recognize both services' numbers for verification codes, which is critical when you're abroad and your bank wants to confirm your identity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use both services?

Absolutely. Some people use DialAnyone for high-volume, low-stakes calls and NomaPhone for important calls where they want reliability. There's no contract with either service, so mixing and matching costs you nothing extra.

### Is DialAnyone's call quality worse because it's cheaper?

Not necessarily. Price doesn't always correlate directly with quality in VoIP. DialAnyone routes calls through their own infrastructure, and many users report good quality. The quality difference, if any, varies by route and conditions. We're not going to claim our quality is better without data to prove it.

### Does NomaPhone plan to add API access?

It's on the roadmap. But it's not available today, and we're not going to sell you on features that don't exist yet.

### Which service is better for receiving 2FA codes from banks?

Both support SMS and 2FA. Both provide numbers that banks recognize for verification codes. This is a tie.

### What if I'm outside the US and need to call a US toll-free number?

Both services handle this. US toll-free numbers (800, 888, 877, etc.) work through both NomaPhone and DialAnyone at their standard per-minute rates.

### Do either service work without internet?

No. Both are VoIP services that require an internet connection. If you're somewhere without reliable WiFi or mobile data, neither service will work. This is a limitation of all browser-based and app-based calling — not specific to either product.
