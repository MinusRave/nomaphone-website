## Greek Mobile Carriers Overview

Three main carriers serve Greece.

### Cosmote

Greece's largest carrier, best nationwide coverage including islands.

**International calling rates:**
- USA/Canada: €1.20-1.50/min
- UK: €1.00-1.30/min
- Germany: €0.90-1.20/min
- EU: Free under roaming rules

**Prepaid plans:**
- Basic: €10/month for 4GB
- Standard: €20/month for 15GB
- Premium: €30/month for unlimited

**Digital nomad verdict:** Best island coverage, expensive internationally.

### Vodafone Greece

Second-largest, good urban coverage.

**International calling rates:**
- USA/Canada: €1.00-1.40/min
- UK: €0.90-1.20/min
- Germany: €0.80-1.10/min

**Prepaid plans:**
- Basic: €12/month for 6GB
- Standard: €20/month for 20GB
- Premium: €30/month for 50GB

**Digital nomad verdict:** Competitive, weaker on smaller islands.

### Wind Hellas

Budget carrier, adequate coverage.

**International calling rates:**
- USA/Canada: €0.90-1.30/min
- UK: €0.80-1.10/min
- Germany: €0.70-1.00/min

**Prepaid plans:**
- Basic: €10/month for 5GB
- Standard: €15/month for 12GB
- Premium: €25/month for unlimited

**Digital nomad verdict:** Cheapest option, check island coverage.

## Seasonal Considerations

Greece's tourist season impacts everything.

### Peak Season (June-September)

**Challenges:**
- Networks congested
- Slower speeds common
- Higher prices everywhere
- Crowded coworking

**Solutions:**
- Call early morning (less traffic)
- Use Ethernet when available
- Have 4G backup
- Book coworking in advance

### Shoulder Season (April-May, October)

**Best time:**
- Good weather
- Less crowded
- Better connectivity
- Lower prices
- Ideal for nomads

### Off-Season (November-March)

**Pros:**
- Fast internet (no tourists)
- Cheap accommodation
- Quiet for calls
- Authentic local life

**Cons:**
- Many businesses closed on islands
- Limited flights to islands
- Cold (by Greek standards)
- Some islands nearly empty

## Time Zone Considerations

Greece uses EET/EEST (UTC+2/+3).

### Calling USA

**East Coast:**
- Greece: 14:00 → USA: 07:00 (EST) or 08:00 (EDT)
- Greece: 20:00 → USA: 13:00 (EST) or 14:00 (EDT)
- Best: 15:00-22:00 Greece

**West Coast:**
- Greece: 14:00 → USA: 04:00 (PST) or 05:00 (PDT)
- Greece: 20:00 → USA: 10:00 (PST) or 11:00 (PDT)
- Best: 18:00-01:00 Greece

### Calling Western Europe

**UK:**
- 2 hours ahead of UK
- Greece: 14:00 → UK: 12:00
- Easy overlap

**Germany/France:**
- 1 hour ahead
- Perfect for business

### Calling Australia

**AEDT:**
- Greece: 14:00 → Sydney: 00:00
- Greece: 09:00 → Sydney: 19:00
- Evening Greece = morning Sydney
