This policy covers the NomaPhone calling service at [app.nomaphone.com](https://app.nomaphone.com) and this website.

## What we collect

No account is needed. We collect the email you enter at checkout (provided by Stripe), payment identifiers, and for each call the number called, time, duration and cost. We never record the content of your calls.

## Why

To provide the service and bill calls, send you your card code and let you recover it, handle refunds and disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations (accounting and tax).

## Who we share it with

Stripe (payments), Twilio (call routing), SendGrid (email), Railway (hosting), PostHog (usage statistics, EU servers). We do not sell your data.

## Usage statistics

In the calling app we measure in aggregate how the service is used (for example how many calls start and to which countries), without cookies and without storing anything on your device. We do not record numbers called, card codes or your session.

This website (nomaphone.com, including the blog) uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser.

## Cookies and storage

In the calling app, your browser keeps your card code and, if you pick one, your language choice. Both stay on your device until you clear them.
